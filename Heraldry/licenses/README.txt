License texts for third-party art in the icon library.

Every library icon names its source, author, license and changes in Library.lua; CREDITS.md
lists them all.

The icons from game-icons.net are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), which lets the credit link to the license
instead of including its text. Their authors are named per icon in CREDITS.md.

When an icon under a license that requires its full text is added (MIT, Apache 2.0, ...),
put that license's text here as <SPDX-id>-<source>.txt, for example MIT-Fluent-Emoji.txt.
